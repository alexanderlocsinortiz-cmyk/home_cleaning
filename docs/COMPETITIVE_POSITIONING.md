# Competitive Positioning

Audit date: 2026-08-27.

## Positioning statement

CleanFlow should position itself as a Valencia City-focused cleaning operations platform that makes booking, assignment, service proof, payment tracking, and follow-up visible in one workflow.

That is a defensible product position. It is not yet proof that CleanFlow is cheaper, faster, or better than a competitor. Those claims require the comparison research listed in the next TODO item.

## Evidence-backed advantages

| Advantage to lead with | Customer benefit | Application evidence | Proof still needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proof-backed service completion | Customers can see before/after service evidence instead of relying only on a verbal promise | Staff must upload before photos to start and after photos to complete; proof is stored with the booking | Real completed jobs, customer acceptance, and consented service examples |

## What is not a safe claim yet

Do not advertise CleanFlow as the cheapest, fastest, highest-rated, safest, 24/7, or guaranteed same-day provider. The repository does not contain competitor quotes, response-time measurements, review volume, completed-job volume, or a service-level guarantee that supports those statements.

Do not call the services fully inclusive yet. The scope audit still requires approved exclusions, capacity limits, equipment rules, condition limits, and real-world evidence.

## Recommended message hierarchy

1. Lead with visibility: “See the estimate, assignment, updates, and completion proof in one booking.”
2. Support it with local practicality: “Book within the configured Valencia City service area, with call/SMS and in-person fallback.”
3. Add continuity: “Request a preferred cleaner or set recurring visits.”
4. Avoid price superiority until the pricing validation and market comparison are complete.

## Proof plan

To turn these feature-backed advantages into competitive advantages, collect:

- three comparable competitor quotes for each major service category;
- booking-to-completion time, assignment success, cancellation, repeat-booking, and complaint metrics;
- consented before/after evidence from each service package;
- customer ratings and acceptance records tied to completed bookings; and
- response-time and outage-contact results for phone, SMS, email, and in-person channels.

## Decision

The product advantages have been identified and tied to implemented workflows. The claim that these advantages outperform competitors remains unproven until the market comparison and operating evidence are completed.
