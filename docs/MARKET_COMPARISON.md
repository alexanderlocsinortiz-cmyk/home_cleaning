## What this comparison supports

CleanFlow’s strongest defensible angle is operational visibility for general cleaning: one record connects the quote, schedule, assignment, payment, service proof, and follow-up. That is more specific than saying “better service,” but it is still a product capability rather than proven market superiority.

The closest public benchmark exposes a serious weakness in CleanFlow’s current positioning: it explains cleaner-hours, supplies, inclusions, extras, custom quotes, and workload limits more clearly than CleanFlow currently does. The service-scope and pricing audits must be completed before CleanFlow claims professional transparency.

The local aircon marketplace shows that customers may already expect fast digital intake, provider verification, availability checks, messaging, and evidence-led marketing. CleanFlow should not claim those ideas are unique without measuring its own response time, provider verification, and completed-job evidence.

## Direct research still required

Before using this table in a proposal, contact at least three local alternatives in Valencia City, Bukidnon and record the same fields:

1. Service packages and exact inclusions.
2. Price unit, minimum charge, area limits, and add-on units.
3. Crew size, expected duration, supplies, and equipment.
4. Service area, booking channel, response time, and schedule availability.
5. Cancellation, re-clean, damage, and customer-acceptance policy.
6. Evidence of actual reviews, completed work, and recurring customers.

Use dated screenshots, written quotations, or recorded calls with permission. Do not treat search snippets, generic directory listings, or competitor marketing claims as independently verified facts.
