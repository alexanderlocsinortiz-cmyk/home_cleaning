# Research and Proposal Comparison

Research date: 2026-08-27.

## Executive decision

Launch CleanFlow as a Valencia City-focused cleaning operations service built around transparent booking and accountable completion. The proposal should sell visibility and reliability: the customer sees the estimate, schedule, assignment, payment state, progress updates, and completion proof in one booking.

Do not launch with a “cheapest,” “fastest,” or “guaranteed full-property completion” promise. The research does not support those claims, and the current scope and pricing rules are not strong enough to defend them.

## Proposal options

| Proposal | Supporting evidence | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lowest-price cleaning service | No approved cost model, competitor price set, or margin evidence | Reject. A price war without unit economics is reckless. |
| Fastest same-day cleaner | No measured response time, guaranteed capacity, or same-day service level | Reject until operational data exists. |
| Full-property cleaning with everything included | Scope audit still lacks exclusions, capacity limits, equipment rules, and condition limits | Reject. This invites disputes and unpaid overwork. |
| Transparent general-cleaning operations in Valencia City | Structured booking, live estimate, local service-area rules, assignment controls, payment tracking, proof uploads, ratings, and offline contact fallback are implemented | Recommended launch position, subject to scope and pricing gates. |
| Premium proof-backed cleaning | Before/after evidence workflow exists, but real completed-job evidence and approved quality standards are missing | Potential phase-two position after the pilot produces proof. |

## Recommended customer segments

| Segment | Current product fit | Launch recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maintained homes needing routine cleaning | Basic Clean and General/Regular Cleaning, recurring plans, add-ons | Primary pilot segment |
| Homes needing a reset or turnover | Deep Clean and Move-in/Move-out Clean | Primary pilot segment after scope sheets are approved |
| Small offices and business spaces | Office Basic, Standard, and Deep packages | Pilot only after office capacity, access, and equipment rules are documented |
| Recently renovated properties | Post Construction Cleaning | Inspection or manual-quote service until construction-material and safety limits are approved |

This segmentation is a product recommendation based on the catalog, not proof of demand. Interview local customers and businesses before spending on acquisition.

## What the market research says

The public benchmark closest to CleanFlow’s general-cleaning offer publishes cleaner-hours, inclusions, extras, supplies, equipment, workload limits, and custom-quote categories. That makes its scope communication a standard CleanFlow must meet, not a weakness to ignore. [Claro’s published pricing and scope](https://www.claroscleaning.ph/cleaning_prices_dumaguete.html)

The Valencia City, Bukidnon aircon marketplace shows that customers may respond to short digital intake, availability checks, provider verification, messaging, and evidence-led marketing. It is an adjacent specialist rather than a general-cleaning competitor, and its public performance and pricing claims should be treated as vendor claims until independently verified. [AirconCleaners Valencia City page](https://airconcleaners.ph/city/valencia-city-bukidnon?lang=en-ph)

Public search did not produce enough standardized general-cleaning price data for Valencia City, Bukidnon. Local primary research is therefore mandatory before making a price or market-share claim.

## Recommended proposal wording

> CleanFlow helps Valencia City households and businesses book cleaning with a clear estimate, a controlled schedule, visible service progress, and proof of completion. Customers can request a preferred cleaner, choose recurring visits, and reach the business through online or offline channels.

This wording is specific, supported by the application, and avoids claims the evidence cannot carry.

## Launch gates

Do not present the proposal as launch-ready until these are complete:

1. Approve an inclusion/exclusion and capacity sheet for every package.
2. Approve the cost model, minimums, overage rules, and commercial rates.
3. Configure the real production contact phone and service-area details.
4. Collect at least one consented evidence set for each service actually marketed.
5. Obtain written or recorded quotes from at least three local general-cleaning alternatives.
6. Define service targets for response time, arrival, completion, complaint handling, and re-clean decisions.

## Pilot scorecard

Track these measures for the first 20 completed bookings:

- quote-to-booking conversion;
- assignment success and time to assignment;
- on-time arrival rate;
- booked versus actual service duration;
- scope exceptions, re-quotes, complaints, and re-cleans;
- payment completion and dispute rate;
- customer rating and acceptance of proof; and
- repeat or recurring-booking rate.

Without this scorecard, “reliable,” “transparent,” and “preferred” remain marketing adjectives rather than demonstrated advantages.
