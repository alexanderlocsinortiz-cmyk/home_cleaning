This matrix is the approval record for the “complete and inclusive” requirement. Draft scope sheets now exist in docs/SERVICE_SCOPE_SHEETS.md. Measurable area and cleaner-count controls are implemented and manually editable, but a service is not approved merely because it has a draft or catalog description. The owner must complete the scope sheet, approve the limits, and attach evidence before the service is marketed as fully inclusive.

## Evidence attachment

For each approved service, attach a dated and consented example with:

1. Before and after photos covering the promised areas.
2. Completed checklist tied to the booking and service type.
3. Property type, floor area, condition, assigned team, and actual duration.
4. Documented exclusions or inaccessible areas.
5. Customer acceptance, rating, or signed completion confirmation.

## Release rule

Until all eight rows have approved scope sheets and evidence, use “standard package features” or “estimate” language. Do not use “everything included,” “complete cleaning,” or equivalent wording as a blanket promise.
