# Draft Service Scope Sheets

Prepared: 2026-08-27.

These are draft customer-service scope sheets based on the current application catalog. Concrete baseline wording is now stored on each service and can be edited in Admin → Services → Edit Service. They are not approved promises until the owner reviews the wording, confirms operating capacity, and signs each sheet before the related package is marketed as complete or inclusive.

## Rules that apply to every package

- The listed tasks are the baseline scope, not an unlimited-work guarantee.
- The property must be safe and reasonably accessible. Locked, occupied, fragile, contaminated, or unsafe areas may be excluded from the visit.
- Mold remediation, pest control, biohazard cleanup, chemical spills, hazardous materials, repairs, painting, and construction work are excluded unless separately approved by a qualified provider.
- Heavy lifting, moving furniture or equipment, ladder/high-access work, and disposal of bulky waste require explicit approval and may require an add-on, inspection, or second visit.
- The baseline company/customer responsibilities for water, power, standard tools, specialty equipment, ladders, and protective equipment are recorded on each service and remain editable.
- Maximum floor area, workload, crew size, and re-quote thresholds have provisional baseline values. The catalog duration alone is not a capacity promise.
- Before/after photos, a completed checklist, recorded exclusions, and customer acceptance should be attached to the booking where consent and site conditions allow.

## Provisional planning assumptions (not approved)

These defaults are for internal planning and pilot preparation only. They are not customer promises. The measurable area and cleaner-count values are stored on each service and can be edited from the admin service editor. Provisional area limits warn and send oversized requests for manual review; approved area limits block oversized self-service bookings. Subjective condition and task decisions still require inspection or an owner-approved scope sheet.

To edit them manually, open Admin → Services → Edit Service and update Maximum Floor Area, Standard Cleaner Count, Scope Status, and the over-limit review checkbox. Cleaner count is currently planning/display metadata; the booking system assigns one provider account and does not automatically create multiple staff assignments. Existing bookings keep their saved duration and price snapshots.

## 1. Basic Clean

- Intended use: Routine cleaning for regularly maintained homes.
- Included baseline: General dusting and surface wipe-down; floor sweeping and mopping; bathroom and kitchen touch-up cleaning.
- Proposed default exclusions: Heavy buildup; detailed grout or degreasing work; appliance interiors; cabinet interiors; windows; walls and ceilings; high-access work; heavy furniture movement; hazardous or specialist cleaning.
- Capacity and duration: Provisional planning assumption is 1 cleaner, 30 sqm, and 60 minutes for routine conditions. Owner approval is still required.
- Add-ons or re-quote triggers: Any excluded task, unusual buildup, excessive clutter, inaccessible area, or workload that cannot fit the booked time.
- Acceptance rule: Customer reviews the completed checklist and records inaccessible or deferred areas before completion.
- Owner decisions required: Supplies/equipment, minimum charge, area cap, exact meaning of “touch-up,” and missed-task/re-clean policy.

## 2. Deep Clean

- Intended use: Homes with buildup, neglected zones, or harder-to-reach areas.
- Included baseline: Detailed bathroom and kitchen scrubbing; focused grime and buildup removal; expanded surface and corner detailing.
- Proposed default exclusions: Mold remediation; pest or biohazard cleanup; appliance interiors; cabinet interiors; windows; wall or ceiling washing; paint, adhesive, or cement removal; high-access work; heavy furniture movement.
- Capacity and duration: Provisional planning assumption is 1 cleaner, 30 sqm, and 180 minutes for moderate buildup. Owner approval is still required.
- Add-ons or re-quote triggers: Specialist sanitation, excluded interiors, severe buildup, unsafe access, or work exceeding the booked time.
- Acceptance rule: Customer confirms completed priority areas and records deferred or inaccessible areas.
- Owner decisions required: Definition of “deep,” supplies/equipment, minimum charge, area cap, inspection threshold, and re-clean policy.

## 3. Move-in/Move-out Clean

- Intended use: Empty-property turnovers, move preparation, and handover cleaning.
- Included baseline: Whole-property cleaning for turnover; cabinet, fixture, and wall-surface attention; move-ready or handover-ready presentation.
- Proposed default exclusions: Removal of bulk construction or abandoned items; repairs; painting; restoration; high-access exterior work; hazardous materials; appliance or cabinet interiors unless specifically listed; damage correction.
- Capacity and duration: Provisional planning assumption is 1 cleaner, 30 sqm, and 240 minutes for an empty property. Owner approval is still required.
- Add-ons or re-quote triggers: Occupied rooms, stored belongings, bulk waste, severe staining, inaccessible areas, or landlord/tenant acceptance standards beyond the checklist.
- Acceptance rule: Customer or authorized representative reviews the turnover checklist and records excluded areas before handover.
- Owner decisions required: Empty-property requirement, interior-window/appliance/cabinet rules, debris responsibility, inspection threshold, and re-clean policy.

## 4. Post Construction Cleaning

- Intended use: Cleanup after renovation, repairs, or newly finished construction work.
- Included baseline: Removal of fine construction dust and debris traces; fixture, ledge, and surface wipe-down; focused cleanup for recently renovated rooms.
- Proposed default exclusions: Bulk debris hauling; wet cement; cured paint, grout, adhesive, or sealant removal; hazardous dust or materials; work in unsafe or unfinished areas; repairs; contractor punch-list work.
- Capacity and duration: Provisional planning assumption is 1 cleaner, 30 sqm, and 240 minutes for light residue only. Owner approval and site-safety review are still required.
- Add-ons or re-quote triggers: Any material removal beyond light residue, contractor waste, unsafe access, active construction, or a site requiring specialist protective equipment.
- Acceptance rule: Authorized site representative reviews cleaned areas, records remaining contractor work, and accepts the checklist.
- Owner decisions required: Construction-debris definition, disposal responsibility, required PPE, contractor boundary, inspection rule, and specialist referral process.

## 5. Office Cleaning (Basic)

- Intended use: Light routine upkeep for small offices.
- Included baseline: Visible surface wiping; floor sweeping and mopping; trash collection; light restroom refresh.
- Proposed default exclusions: Confidential-file handling; electronics disassembly; furniture or equipment relocation; high-access work; heavy grease or buildup; exterior windows; bulky or hazardous waste.
- Capacity and duration: Provisional planning assumption is 1 cleaner, 30 sqm, and 120 minutes for routine occupancy and trash volume. Owner approval is still required.
- Add-ons or re-quote triggers: Extra restroom/pantry detail, excessive trash, occupied work zones, locked areas, equipment movement, or work beyond the booked time.
- Acceptance rule: Authorized office contact reviews the checklist and records inaccessible or deferred zones.
- Owner decisions required: Consumables, access/security procedure, desk and equipment handling, closing duties, area cap, and recurring-service standard.

## 6. Office Cleaning (Standard)

- Intended use: Offices, storefronts, and business-ready workspaces.
- Included baseline: Reception and workstation cleaning routines; restroom and pantry area sanitation; business-hours friendly cleaning workflow.
- Proposed default exclusions: Confidential records; electronics handling beyond exterior wiping; furniture or equipment relocation; high-access work; specialist sanitation; exterior windows; bulky or hazardous waste.
- Capacity and duration: Provisional planning assumption is 1 cleaner, 30 sqm, and 180 minutes for routine occupancy. Owner approval is still required.
- Add-ons or re-quote triggers: Extra pantry/restroom workload, heavy buildup, blocked workstations, locked rooms, equipment movement, or work beyond the booked time.
- Acceptance rule: Authorized office contact reviews the checklist and confirms the areas completed during the access window.
- Owner decisions required: Security/access rules, desk and equipment limits, consumables, frequency standard, area cap, and re-clean policy.

## 7. Office Cleaning (Deep)

- Intended use: Periodic office resets and heavier buildup.
- Included baseline: Detailed workstation and high-touch cleaning; restroom and pantry deep sanitation; heavier floor and surface detailing.
- Proposed default exclusions: Electronics disassembly; confidential-file handling; furniture or equipment relocation; high-access work; mold, biohazard, pest, or specialist sanitation; exterior windows; hazardous or bulky waste.
- Capacity and duration: Provisional planning assumption is 1 cleaner, 30 sqm, and 240 minutes for moderate buildup. Owner approval is still required.
- Add-ons or re-quote triggers: Severe buildup, specialist sanitation, blocked work areas, equipment movement, unsafe access, or work beyond the booked time.
- Acceptance rule: Authorized office contact reviews the deep-clean checklist and records deferred or inaccessible zones.
- Owner decisions required: Confirm that PHP 60/sqm applies to the intended condition range, plus equipment, furniture, electronics, area-cap, inspection, and re-quote rules.

## 8. General/Regular Cleaning

- Intended use: One-time or recurring routine home upkeep.
- Included baseline: General dusting, sweeping, and mopping; routine kitchen and bathroom refresh; up to four hours per standard session.
- Proposed default exclusions: Deep-cleaning buildup; appliance or cabinet interiors; windows; walls and ceilings; heavy lifting; bulky-waste removal; mold, pest, biohazard, chemical, or specialist work.
- Capacity and duration: Provisional planning assumption is 1 cleaner, 30 sqm, and up to four hours for routine workload. Owner approval is still required.
- Add-ons or re-quote triggers: Any excluded task, excessive clutter, severe condition, inaccessible area, or work that cannot fit within the session.
- Acceptance rule: Customer agrees on priority tasks at the start, reviews the checklist at the end, and records deferred work before completion.
- Owner decisions required: Select the rule for PHP 500-800, supplies/equipment, room or area cap, overage rate, recurring-service expectation, and rebooking policy.

## Approval checklist

Before marking a service approved, record:

1. Owner-approved inclusions and exclusions.
2. Crew size, productive cleaner-hours, maximum area/workload, and duration.
3. Customer/company responsibility for supplies, equipment, access, and safety.
4. Add-on, inspection, re-quote, overage, and second-visit rules.
5. Customer acceptance wording and the checklist used on the job.
6. At least one dated, consented evidence set tied to the service and booking.
7. Approver, approval date, effective date, and geographic/property limits.
